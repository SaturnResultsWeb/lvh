Dining at La Ville

Good Food, Good Times

Our Restaurant serves a delicious variety of food for breakfast, brunch and lunch. We always use the best, locally sourced ingredients wherever possible — because fresh island food just tastes better.

Whether you’re fuelling up for a day of island exploration or settling in for a lazy terrace brunch with an Aperol Spritz, we’ve got you covered.

£5 Aperol Spritz all day, every day. Join us on our sunny terrace and watch the world go by.
