About Food Dude

Great Food, Anywhere

The Food Dude is La Ville’s licensed mobile catering facility. You will find us at all major island events — bringing the flavour wherever the fun is!

Keep your eyes peeled at Alderney Week, Bunkers Parties, Sports Matches, Hill Climb Weekend, Bonfire Night and the Christmas Market.

We can also be booked for outside events including weddings, garden parties and private functions — get in touch to book.
