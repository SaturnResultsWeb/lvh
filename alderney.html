Discover Alderney

From stunning clifftops to crystal-clear beaches — the hidden gem of the Channel Islands awaits.

The Island

A Place Like No Other

From stunning views to pleasant walks, Alderney has a wonderful selection of nature and activities. With a slow pace of life, it’s perfect for a relaxing holiday or an escape from the bustle of city life.

Alderney has a very visual historical past that spans over many centuries — from ancient occupation to the Second World War fortifications that still stand today.

Alderney’s beaches are boasted as the best in the Channel Islands with crystal clear waters and soft sand. Whether you want a relaxing holiday, or a holiday packed with adventures — Alderney has it all.

Visit Alderney
Things to Do

Island Highlights

Alderney Wildlife Centre, Alderney Museum, shops and restaurants are all within a few minutes’ walking distance from La Ville’s front door.

Braye Beach

The island’s most popular beach, with a beautiful stretch of golden sand, calm waters and a fantastic beach bar. Perfect for swimming, paddling and sunbathing.

Alderney Wildlife Trust

Home to rare puffins, gannets and the famous blonde hedgehogs — Alderney’s wildlife is extraordinary and unlike anywhere else in the British Isles.

Gannet Rock

A spectacular wander from the hotel to see thousands of gannets nesting — an unforgettable and uniquely Alderney experience that takes your breath away.

Alderney Museum

Explore Alderney’s fascinating history spanning many centuries — from ancient habitation to German occupation in World War II and beyond.

Lighthouse Ride

Hire a bike and ride out to the lighthouse for breathtaking panoramic views across the Channel — a must-do for every visitor to the island.

St. Anne’s Church

Known as the Cathedral of the Channel Islands — one of the finest Victorian buildings in the archipelago, visible right from La Ville’s front door.

WWII Fortifications

Alderney has some of the most intact German WWII fortifications in the British Isles — a haunting and historically significant part of the island’s landscape.

Water Taxis & Boat Trips

Explore the surrounding waters and nearby islands by water taxi or boat trip — an exciting way to see Alderney from a different perspective.

Alderney Week

The island’s biggest annual celebration, held in August — a wonderful week of events, races, music and festivities enjoyed by islanders and visitors alike.

Getting to Alderney

How to Reach Us

Alderney is easy to reach from the UK and mainland Europe — smaller than you’d think and more accessible than ever!

By Air

Aurigny Air Services fly to Alderney from Guernsey and Southampton. Finistair also operate flights from France.

By Sea

Water Taxi CI and other operators run services from Guernsey and surrounding areas.

From the airport or harbour

Local taxis are available. See our contact page for taxi numbers.
