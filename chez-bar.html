About The Chez

Where the Party's At

The largest bar on Alderney, with regular DJs and live music. We serve the best selection of rum, gin, draft lager and real ales on the island.

The perfect venue for celebrations — with two 50" screens showing Sky TV and TNT Sports. It's the only place to be to watch every sporting event.

🥩 Meat Draw every Friday from 6:30pm with our friends at The Alderney Farm Shop — raising money for local charities.

🎤 Karaoke Night — an island favourite, every Sunday from 9pm. You won't want to miss it!
